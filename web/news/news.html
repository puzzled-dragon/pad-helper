Welcome on PDHelper

This project was started years ago (almost ten years ago now), when I was still a student in computer science.

Time flies, and life happens... I don't have as much time as I had, and I don't even play that much of Puzzle and Dragons anymore (Still login in though ! And I'm almost at 3700 cumulative login days !)

As the years went, I always found the time (with a lot of delay... I'm sorry for all the people who contacted me via email and that I let hanging for weeks) to fix bugs, and maintain the app (each release of Android was a lot of pain, since I don't code in Java/don't program for Android in my daily life). But this time has come to an end... I can't keep up with the Android releases and since the SDK 34 (Android 14), I didn't manage to properly upgrade the app (It's still working on a lot of devices, but it will soon cease to do so).

The app will still be working while it can, but I cancelled all the remaining subscriptions. And the server will be shutdown in january (when no more subscriptions are running).


I'm forever grateful for all the people that supported this project (either with subscriptions, which helped pay for the server, or simply emails). It made me feel proud, and like I had actually accomplished something, and I loved doing a project that was useful for at least a handful of people.

So, thank you, to all of you puzzle and dragons lovers ! It's been a pleasure, and keep puzzling !

As a side note, I'm open to sharing the code (app & server), but beware that it is a hot mess (be kind, remember that the majority of it was done when a I was a student, so... yeah it's not good code).
